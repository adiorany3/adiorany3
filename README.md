## ⭐ Featured Open-Source Projects

### 🌾 RansumRuminansia  
**Open-source livestock feed formulation software for ruminant nutrition.**

RansumRuminansia is designed for **cattle, sheep, and goat ration formulation**, nutrient evaluation, feed balancing, and least-cost optimization.

**Keywords:**  
`Livestock Feed Formulation` · `Ruminant Nutrition` · `Animal Nutrition Software` · `Ration Optimization` · `Agricultural Software` · `Linear Programming`

---

### 🥛 RansumSapiPerah  
**Dairy cattle nutrition planning and feed formulation software.**

RansumSapiPerah helps with **dairy cow ration planning, nutrient requirement calculation, feed ingredient evaluation, and least-cost dairy feed formulation**.

**Keywords:**  
`Dairy Cattle Nutrition` · `Dairy Feed Formulation` · `Livestock Management Software` · `Agricultural Decision Support System`

---

### 📊 OneWay  
**One-Way ANOVA software for research and experimental data analysis.**

OneWay provides tools for **one-way analysis of variance, post-hoc testing, effect size calculation, and statistical interpretation**.

**Keywords:**  
`One-Way ANOVA Software` · `Statistical Analysis` · `Research Software` · `Experimental Design` · `Data Analysis`

---

### 🔬 TwoWay  
**Two-Way ANOVA software with interaction analysis.**

TwoWay is focused on **two-factor experimental design, interaction effects, statistical modeling, and research data interpretation**.

**Keywords:**  
`Two-Way ANOVA` · `Interaction Analysis` · `Statistical Modeling` · `Research Data Analysis`

---

### 📈 T-Test  
**T-Test calculator and statistical testing software.**

This project supports **one-sample T-Test, independent sample T-Test, confidence intervals, effect size analysis, and statistical reporting**.

**Keywords:**  
`T-Test Calculator` · `Statistical Testing` · `Confidence Interval` · `Effect Size` · `Research Statistics`

---

### 🐄 Karkas  
**Livestock carcass and non-carcass estimation software.**

Karkas helps estimate **carcass weight, non-carcass components, and livestock production outputs** based on scientific production formulas.

**Keywords:**  
`Livestock Management Software` · `Carcass Estimation` · `Animal Production` · `Agricultural Tools`
