## 👨‍💻 About Me

Research Software Engineer specializing in **AgriTech solutions** for livestock industries. I develop tools that bridge animal science research, data analytics, and artificial intelligence.

**Core Expertise:**
- 🐮 **Livestock Nutrition Systems** — Feed formulation, cost optimization, ingredient databases
- 📊 **Farm Management Software** — Recording systems, monitoring dashboards, decision support tools
- 🔬 **Scientific Computing** — Statistical analysis (ANOVA), reproducible research workflows, data utilities
- 🤖 **Applied AI for Agriculture** — Computer vision, predictive modeling, automation solutions
- 🌾 **IoT for Smart Farming** — Sensor systems, environmental monitoring, automated controls

My work transforms research data, statistical methods, and ML models into **accessible software** for researchers, educators, farmers, and industry practitioners.

**Research Focus:** Animal genetics · Bioinformatics · Big data in livestock production · Decision support systems
